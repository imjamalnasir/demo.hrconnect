Help Center

How can we help?

Quick answers to common questions about using HR Connect. Inside the app, the AI assistant can help too.

Getting started

What are the Me and People workspaces?

Me is your own workspace: your profile, payslips, leave, loans, expenses and policies.

People is where HR and admins manage the company. You only see the menus your role has permission for.

I didn't get my invite email. What should I do?

Ask your HR team to resend your invite from the employee directory. They can also send you new login details.

I forgot my password

On the login page choose Forgot password. We'll email you a code to reset it.

Employees

How do I add an employee?

Go to People → Employees → Directory and choose Add employee. The wizard collects personal, job, pay and bank details, then sends an invite.

How do I update my own details?

Go to Me → My Profile. What you can edit depends on your role's permissions.

Where is the org chart?

Open Org Chart from the Employees menu. It's built from each employee's manager.

Leave

How do I request leave?

Go to Me → My Leave, choose New request, pick the type and dates and submit. Business days are counted using your weekend days.

Can I edit or cancel a request?

Yes, while it's still pending. Approved requests can't be deleted.

How do I approve leave?

Go to People → Leave Management, open a pending request and approve, or reject with a reason.

Where can I see balances?

Switch to the Balances view to see allocated, used, carried-forward and remaining days.

Payroll

How do I create a payroll setup?

Go to People → Payroll → Setup and choose New Setup. Enter name, country and currency, then components, leave rules and final settlement. Once a setup is in use, only its name can change.

How do I run payroll?

Go to Payroll Runs, choose New Payroll Run, pick the setup and month. The run is calculated straight away.

Add any one-off earnings or deductions, submit for approval, then mark it paid once approved. Payslips are issued when the run is paid.

Why can't I submit a run?

A run can't be submitted if it has no employees, duplicate lines, zero gross pay or negative net pay.

How do I get the bank file?

Open the run and export the bank advice as CSV or XLSX. Approvers can see it while the run is pending.

Where are my payslips?

Go to Me → Payslips, open the month and choose Download to save it as PDF.

Loans, expenses & advances

How do I submit an expense claim?

Go to Me → Expenses, add a claim and upload the receipt. Use Auto scan to let AI fill in the details, check them and submit.

When will I be reimbursed?

Approved claims are paid in the next payroll run.

How do I request an advance or a loan?

Use Me → Advances or Me → Loans. Once approved, the money is paid through payroll and loan installments are deducted each month.

Offboarding

How do I start a separation?

From the directory or Separations, choose Initiate separation and enter type, reason and last working day. Reassign any direct reports first.

What are the steps?

Request, approval, exit checklist, final settlement, then exited. The employee is marked exited when the payroll run for their last month is paid.

Settings & permissions

How do I control what people can see?

Go to Settings → User Permissions. Give each role permissions per module and action, and override them for individual users if needed.

How do I publish a policy?

Go to Settings → Policies and upload the document. Employees see it under My Policies and can ask the AI assistant about it.

Raise a ticket from Support inside HR Connect, or contact us.
