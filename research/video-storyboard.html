Production notes

Look and sound

  • Colours: plum #6C3845, deep plum #2E1820, soft pink #F6EEF0. Green #0F8A5F only for "Approved" states.
  • Type: Inter, bold for headlines, with the second half of each headline in the soft pink tint.
  • Motion: smooth, short eases (300–500 ms). UI cards slide up 24px and fade in; list rows appear one after another.
  • Music: light, modern electronic or piano at about 100–110 BPM. It builds into the products section and resolves on the end card.
  • Voiceover: warm, confident and unhurried. One voice, no jargon.
  • Sound effects: soft clicks on approvals and a gentle "whoosh" for the receipt scan only. Keep them subtle.

Formats and delivery

  • Master: 16:9, 1920×1080, for the website, YouTube and demos.
  • LinkedIn feed: 1:1 (1080×1080) or 4:5 (1080×1350). Keep key UI inside the centre square when animating the master.
  • Stories and Reels: 9:16 (1080×1920) for the 15-second cut.
  • Captions: burn them in. Most social video plays muted, and the on-screen text should still tell the story without sound.
  • UI: use the real product screens where possible. The names and figures in these frames are the same sample data used on the website.
  • End card: hold for at least 3 seconds with the URL hrconnect.ai/book-a-demo.
