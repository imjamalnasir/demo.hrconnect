Before sending these out

  • Contact details use the site's placeholders, hrconnect.ai and hello@hrconnect.ai. Update them in _build/content.py and in the document templates once they're final.
  • There are no customer logos or testimonials yet, so the "Proof" slide shows controls a buyer can check in a demo. Add real customer results there when you have them.
  • Pricing says "Custom quote", like the website. Add prices when they're set.
  • To change the wording, edit research/sales-docs/src/*.tpl.html and run python3 research/sales-docs/src/build_docs.py. It rebuilds both PDFs and these previews (needs Google Chrome on a Mac).
