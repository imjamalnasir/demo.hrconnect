Master Subscription Agreement

The main terms for every customer: the service, fees, customer data, liability and termination. Each Order Form sits under it.

Sample template. Replace everything in [brackets], delete what doesn't apply, and have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer in your jurisdiction before use. It is not legal advice.

This Master Subscription Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made on [date] between:

(1) [HR Connect legal entity name], a company registered in [country] under number [company number], whose registered office is at [registered address] (“HR Connect”)

(2) [Customer legal name], a company registered in [country] under number [company number], whose registered office is at [registered address] (“Customer”)

Each a “party” and together the “parties”. The parties agree as follows.

1.Definitions

In this Agreement:

“Services” means the HRConnect.AI cloud HR and payroll software described in an Order Form, including updates, and related support.

“Order Form” means an ordering document signed by both parties that refers to this Agreement.

“Users” means the Customer's employees, contractors and administrators who are given access to the Services.

“Customer Data” means all data, including personal data, that the Customer or its Users enter into or upload to the Services.

“Documentation” means HR Connect's then-current user guides and help articles for the Services.

“Subscription Term” means the period stated in the Order Form, including any renewals.

2.The Services

2.1HR Connect will make the Services available to the Customer during the Subscription Term in line with this Agreement, the Order Form and the Documentation.

2.2The Customer may allow its Users to access the Services for its internal business purposes. The Customer is responsible for its Users' acts and omissions.

2.3HR Connect may update the Services from time to time. It will not make an update that materially reduces the overall functionality of the Services the Customer has subscribed to during the Subscription Term.

2.4Support and service levels are set out in the Service Level Agreement, if one is attached to the Order Form.

3.Customer responsibilities

3.1The Customer will: (a) keep the data it enters accurate and up to date; (b) keep login details secure and tell HR Connect promptly of any unauthorised access; and (c) use the Services only in line with applicable law and the Documentation.

3.2The Customer will not: (a) resell, sublicense or make the Services available to third parties; (b) copy, modify, reverse engineer or attempt to access the source code of the Services; (c) use the Services to store or send unlawful or malicious content; or (d) interfere with the security or performance of the Services.

3.3The Services help the Customer run HR and payroll administration. They do not provide legal, tax, financial or employment advice. The Customer remains responsible for its employment decisions, for checking payroll results before approving them, and for making salary payments and tax filings. The Services produce a bank payment file; they do not send payments.

4.Fees and payment

4.1The Customer will pay the fees set out in the Order Form. Fees are priced per employee per month and invoiced [monthly / annually] in advance, unless the Order Form says otherwise.

4.2Invoices are due within [30] days of the invoice date, in the currency stated in the Order Form.

4.3Fees exclude taxes. The Customer will pay any applicable sales, value-added or similar taxes, other than taxes on HR Connect's income.

4.4If an undisputed invoice is more than [15] days overdue, HR Connect may, after giving at least [10] days' written notice, suspend the Services until payment is received.

4.5HR Connect may change its fees with effect from the start of a renewal term by giving at least [60] days' written notice before that renewal.

5.Customer Data

5.1The Customer owns all Customer Data. The Customer grants HR Connect a limited right to process Customer Data only as needed to provide, secure and support the Services and as otherwise set out in this Agreement.

5.2Where HR Connect processes personal data on the Customer's behalf, the Data Processing Agreement between the parties applies and forms part of this Agreement.

5.3HR Connect will keep appropriate technical and organisational measures in place to protect Customer Data, as described in the Data Processing Agreement.

5.4The Customer can export its Customer Data from the Services at any time during the Subscription Term.

5.5After this Agreement ends, HR Connect will keep Customer Data available for export for [30] days and will then delete it within [90] days, unless law requires HR Connect to keep it.

6.AI features

6.1The Services include AI features, such as an assistant that answers questions and drafts content. AI output may be incomplete or inaccurate. The Customer is responsible for reviewing AI output before relying on it or acting on it.

6.2AI features only use the data that the requesting User is allowed to see under the Customer's permission settings.

6.3HR Connect will not use Customer Data to train AI models that are made available to other customers [unless the Customer agrees in writing].

7.Confidentiality

7.1“Confidential Information” means information disclosed by one party to the other that is marked as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential. Customer Data is the Customer's Confidential Information.

7.2Each party will use the other's Confidential Information only to perform this Agreement, will protect it with at least reasonable care, and will disclose it only to its employees, contractors and advisers who need to know it and are bound by similar duties.

7.3These duties do not apply to information that is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving party, was already known to it, is received lawfully from a third party, or is independently developed. A party may disclose Confidential Information where required by law, after giving the other party notice where lawful.

8.Intellectual property

8.1HR Connect and its licensors own all rights in the Services, the Documentation and any improvements to them. No rights are granted to the Customer other than those expressly set out in this Agreement.

8.2If the Customer gives feedback or suggestions about the Services, HR Connect may use them without restriction or payment.

9.Warranties

9.1Each party warrants that it has the authority to enter into this Agreement.

9.2HR Connect warrants that the Services will perform materially in line with the Documentation. If they do not, the Customer's remedy is for HR Connect to use reasonable efforts to correct the failure, or, if it cannot do so within a reasonable time, for either party to terminate the affected Order Form and for HR Connect to refund any prepaid fees for the rest of the Subscription Term.

9.3Except as stated in this Agreement, the Services are provided “as is” and HR Connect gives no other warranties, to the extent permitted by law.

10.Indemnities

10.1HR Connect will defend the Customer against any third-party claim that the Services infringe that third party's intellectual property rights, and will pay any damages finally awarded or agreed in settlement.

10.2The Customer will defend HR Connect against any third-party claim arising from Customer Data or from the Customer's use of the Services in breach of this Agreement or the law, and will pay any damages finally awarded or agreed in settlement.

10.3The indemnified party must notify the other party promptly of the claim, let it control the defence and settlement, and give reasonable help at its cost.

11.Limitation of liability

11.1Neither party is liable for any indirect or consequential loss, or for loss of profits, revenue, goodwill or data, however caused.

11.2Each party's total liability under or in connection with this Agreement in any 12-month period is limited to the fees paid and payable by the Customer under this Agreement in the 12 months before the event giving rise to the claim.

11.3Nothing in this Agreement limits liability for fraud, for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for the Customer's payment obligations, for the indemnities in clause 10, or for anything else that cannot be limited by law.

12.Term and termination

12.1This Agreement starts on the date above and continues while any Order Form is in force.

12.2Each Order Form renews automatically for successive periods equal to its initial term, unless either party gives at least [30] days' written notice of non-renewal before the end of the current term.

12.3Either party may terminate this Agreement or an Order Form by written notice if the other party: (a) commits a material breach that is not remedied within [30] days of written notice; or (b) becomes insolvent or ceases to do business.

12.4When this Agreement ends: the Customer's access to the Services ends, the Customer pays any fees due up to the end date, and clause 5.5 (data export and deletion) applies. Clauses that by their nature should survive termination will survive.

13.General

13.1This Agreement is governed by the laws of [governing law jurisdiction]. The courts of [jurisdiction for disputes] have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising out of or in connection with it, unless the parties agree in writing to resolve a dispute by [mediation / arbitration under named rules].

13.2Notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses in the Order Form, or to another address a party notifies. Notices by email are valid when sent to the contact named in the Order Form.

13.3Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's written consent, which will not be unreasonably withheld, except to a successor in a merger or sale of all or substantially all of its business.

13.4Neither party is liable for a delay or failure caused by events beyond its reasonable control.

13.5This Agreement, the Order Forms and the Data Processing Agreement are the entire agreement between the parties on their subject. If they conflict, the order of precedence is: (a) the Order Form; (b) the Data Processing Agreement; (c) this Agreement; (d) the Documentation.

13.6Changes to this Agreement must be in writing and signed by both parties. This Agreement may be signed electronically and in counterparts.
