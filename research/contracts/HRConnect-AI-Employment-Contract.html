This Employment Contract (the “Contract”) is made on [date] between:

(1) [Company name], a company registered in [country] under number [company number], whose registered office is at [registered address] (“Company”)

(2) [Employee full name] of [employee address] (“Employee”).

1.Start of employment

1.1The Employee's employment starts on [start date]. [The Employee's previous employment with [previous employer] does not count towards continuous employment.]

1.2This is a [permanent / fixed-term] contract. [If fixed term: it ends on [end date] unless terminated earlier under clause 16 or extended in writing.]

2.Job title and duties

2.1The Employee is employed as [job title] in the [department] department, reporting to [manager job title].

2.2The Employee will carry out the duties set out in the job description and any other reasonable duties the Company assigns that fit the Employee's skills and role.

2.3The Employee will follow the Company's lawful and reasonable instructions and policies, and will act in good faith in the Company's interests.

3.Place of work

The Employee's normal place of work is [office address / remote / hybrid, at least [number] days a week at the office]. The Company may require reasonable business travel.

4.Probation

4.1The first [3] months of employment are a probation period. The Company may extend it by up to [3] months by written notice before it ends.

4.2During probation, either party may end the employment by giving [1] week's written notice.

5.Working hours

5.1The Employee's normal working hours are [40] hours a week, [09:00 to 18:00, Monday to Friday], with [1] hour unpaid break each day.

5.2The Employee may need to work additional hours to perform the role. [Overtime is paid at [rate] / is not separately paid and is reflected in the salary], subject to applicable law.

6.Salary

6.1The Employee's gross salary is $[0.00] per month ($[0.00] per year).

6.2Salary is paid monthly in arrears on or before [the last working day] of each month, by bank transfer to the account the Employee records in the Company's HR system.

6.3The Company will make any deductions required by law and any deductions the Employee agrees to in writing, such as repayments of a salary advance or loan.

6.4Payslips are available to the Employee through the self-service area of the Company's HR system.

6.5Salary is reviewed [annually]. A review does not guarantee an increase.

7.Bonus and benefits

7.1The Employee [is / is not] eligible for the [bonus scheme name], under its rules in force from time to time. Any bonus is discretionary unless the scheme rules say otherwise.

7.2The Employee is entitled to the following benefits: [health insurance, allowances, pension or retirement plan, other]. The Company may change benefit providers or terms on reasonable notice.

8.Expenses

The Company will reimburse reasonable expenses the Employee properly incurs for work, in line with the Company's expense policy. Claims must be submitted through the Company's HR system with receipts within [30] days.

9.Annual leave

9.1The Employee is entitled to [20] working days' paid annual leave in each leave year, plus public holidays observed at [location]. The leave year runs from [1 January to 31 December].

9.2Leave is requested and approved through the Company's HR system. Up to [5] days of unused leave may be carried over to the next leave year [and must be used within [3] months].

9.3On termination, the Employee will be paid for accrued but untaken leave, or the Company will deduct pay for leave taken in excess of accrual, as allowed by law.

10.Sick leave and other leave

10.1If the Employee is unable to work because of illness, they must tell their manager as early as possible on the first day of absence and record the absence in the HR system. A medical certificate is required for absences of more than [2] consecutive days.

10.2The Employee is entitled to [number] days' paid sick leave a year, and to statutory sick pay where it applies.

10.3The Employee is entitled to parental, bereavement and other leave as required by law and as set out in the Company's leave policy.

11.Confidentiality

11.1During and after employment, the Employee will not use or disclose any confidential information about the Company, its customers, suppliers or employees, except as needed to do their job or as required by law.

11.2Confidential information includes business plans, finances, customer lists, pricing, product designs, source code, personnel and salary information, and any information marked or treated as confidential.

12.Intellectual property

12.1All intellectual property created by the Employee in the course of their employment belongs to the Company. To the extent it does not belong to the Company automatically, the Employee assigns it to the Company.

12.2The Employee will sign any documents reasonably needed to confirm the Company's ownership, during or after employment.

13.Outside activities and conflicts

13.1During employment, the Employee will not take on other paid work or any activity that conflicts with the Company's interests without the Company's prior written consent, which will not be unreasonably withheld.

13.2The Employee will tell the Company promptly of any actual or potential conflict of interest.

14.Restrictions after employment

14.1For [6] months after employment ends, the Employee will not solicit or try to hire any employee of the Company with whom they worked in the last [12] months of employment.

14.2For [6] months after employment ends, the Employee will not solicit business from any customer of the Company with whom they dealt in the last [12] months of employment.

14.3[Include any non-compete only where enforceable in the relevant jurisdiction, and limit it in time, area and scope.]

15.Personal data

The Company processes the Employee's personal data for employment purposes, as described in its employee privacy notice. The Employee will keep their personal details in the HR system up to date.

16.Notice and termination

16.1After probation, either party may end the employment by giving [1] month's written notice, or the longer notice required by law.

16.2The Company may pay the Employee's basic salary in lieu of all or part of the notice period, or require the Employee not to attend work during all or part of it while continuing to receive salary and benefits.

16.3The Company may end the employment immediately without notice or pay in lieu in case of gross misconduct or a serious breach of this Contract, as allowed by law.

16.4[End-of-service or severance payments are made as required by applicable law.]

17.Return of property

When employment ends, or earlier if asked, the Employee will return all Company property, including equipment, access cards, documents and data, and will not keep any copies.

18.Disciplinary and grievance

The Company's disciplinary and grievance procedures are set out in the employee handbook. They do not form part of this Contract.

19.General

19.1This Contract, together with the Company's policies as amended from time to time, is the entire agreement between the parties about the employment. Where a policy conflicts with this Contract, this Contract prevails.

19.2Changes to this Contract must be agreed in writing, except for minor changes to policies that the Company notifies to the Employee.

19.3This Contract is governed by the laws of [country / state], and its courts have exclusive jurisdiction.
