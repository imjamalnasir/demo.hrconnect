This Data Processing Agreement (“DPA”) is made on [date] between:

(1) [Customer legal name], a company registered in [country] under number [company number], whose registered office is at [registered address] (“Customer”)

(2) [HR Connect legal entity name], a company registered in [country] under number [company number], whose registered office is at [registered address] (“HR Connect”)

It forms part of the Master Subscription Agreement between the parties (the “Agreement”).

1.Definitions and roles

1.1“Data Protection Law” means all data protection and privacy laws that apply to the processing of personal data under the Agreement. “Personal data”, “processing”, “controller”, “processor” and “data subject” have the meanings given in Data Protection Law, or their nearest equivalents.

1.2The Customer is the controller of the personal data in Customer Data and HR Connect is its processor. Annex 1 describes the processing.

1.3The Customer is responsible for having a lawful basis for the processing and for giving data subjects any required notices.

2.Instructions

2.1HR Connect will process personal data only on the Customer's documented instructions, including with regard to transfers, unless law requires otherwise. The Agreement, this DPA and the Customer's use and configuration of the Services are the Customer's instructions.

2.2HR Connect will tell the Customer if, in its opinion, an instruction breaks Data Protection Law.

3.Confidentiality of personnel

HR Connect will ensure that people authorised to process personal data are bound by confidentiality duties and only access it as needed to provide the Services.

4.Security

4.1HR Connect will implement and maintain the technical and organisational measures in Annex 2, appropriate to the risk of the processing.

4.2HR Connect may update these measures, as long as the update does not reduce the overall level of protection.

5.Sub-processors

5.1The Customer gives general authorisation for HR Connect to use sub-processors. The current list is in Annex 3.

5.2HR Connect will give the Customer at least [30] days' notice before adding or replacing a sub-processor. The Customer may object on reasonable data protection grounds within that period. If the parties cannot resolve the objection, the Customer may terminate the affected Services and receive a refund of prepaid fees for the remaining term.

5.3HR Connect will impose data protection terms on each sub-processor that are no less protective than this DPA and remains responsible for its sub-processors' performance.

6.Data subject requests and assistance

6.1Taking into account the nature of the processing, HR Connect will help the Customer respond to requests from data subjects to exercise their rights, mainly through features of the Services that let the Customer view, correct, export and delete personal data.

6.2If HR Connect receives a request directly from a data subject, it will pass it to the Customer and will not respond itself unless the Customer instructs it to.

6.3HR Connect will give reasonable help with data protection impact assessments and consultations with regulators, where these relate to the Services.

7.Personal data breaches

7.1HR Connect will notify the Customer without undue delay, and in any case within [48] hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Customer Data.

7.2The notice will describe, as far as known, the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of data subjects and records concerned, the likely consequences and the measures taken or proposed. HR Connect will provide further information as it becomes available.

8.International transfers

HR Connect will host Customer Data in [hosting region]. HR Connect will transfer personal data to another country only where Data Protection Law allows it, using any safeguards that law requires.

9.Deletion and return

When the Agreement ends, the Customer can export its personal data as set out in the Agreement. HR Connect will then delete it, including copies, within [90] days, unless law requires it to keep some of it. Backups are deleted on their normal cycle of no more than [35] days.

10.Audits

10.1HR Connect will make available the information reasonably needed to show compliance with this DPA, including [security questionnaires, policies and third-party audit reports, where available].

10.2If that information is not enough, the Customer may carry out an audit no more than once a year, on at least [30] days' notice, during business hours, at its own cost, and under confidentiality terms. Audits must not disrupt HR Connect's business or access other customers' data.

11.Liability and term

11.1Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations and exclusions in the Agreement.

11.2This DPA lasts as long as HR Connect processes personal data for the Customer under the Agreement.
