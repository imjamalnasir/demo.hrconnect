This Service Level Agreement (“SLA”) is made on [date] between [HR Connect legal entity name] (“HR Connect”) and [Customer legal name] (“Customer”). It forms part of the Master Subscription Agreement between the parties (the “Agreement”).

1.Availability

1.1HR Connect will make the Services available [99.5]% of the time in each calendar month (the “Availability Target”).

1.2Availability is calculated as: (total minutes in the month − minutes of Downtime) ÷ total minutes in the month × 100. “Downtime” means time when Users cannot log in to or use the core features of the Services, as confirmed by HR Connect's monitoring.

2.Exclusions

Downtime does not include time caused by:

scheduled maintenance announced at least [48] hours in advance and carried out within [the maintenance window, for example 22:00–04:00 in the Customer's time zone], up to [4] hours a month;

emergency security maintenance;

the Customer's systems, internet connection, configuration or misuse of the Services;

events beyond HR Connect's reasonable control, as described in the Agreement;

features labelled beta, preview or trial.

3.Support

3.1Support is available by [in-app chat and email to support@hrconnect.ai] during [Monday to Friday, 09:00–18:00 in the Customer's main time zone], excluding public holidays at [location] (“Business Hours”). Severity 1 issues are handled [24/7].

3.2HR Connect will respond to support requests within the times below, measured from when the request is received:

SeverityMeaningFirst responseUpdates
1 – CriticalServices unavailable for all Users, or a payroll run is blocked close to the pay date[1 hour]Every [2] hours until resolved
2 – HighA major feature does not work and there is no workaround[4 Business Hours]Every [business day]
3 – NormalA feature does not work as expected but there is a workaround[1 business day]As progress is made
4 – LowQuestions, how-to requests and feature suggestions[3 business days]As needed

3.3The Customer will give the information reasonably needed to reproduce and diagnose an issue, and will name up to [3] people as support contacts.

4.Service credits

4.1If availability in a month falls below the Availability Target, the Customer may claim a service credit, calculated as a percentage of the monthly subscription fee for that month:

Monthly availabilityService credit
Below [99.5]% but at least [99.0]%[5]%
Below [99.0]% but at least [98.0]%[10]%
Below [98.0]%[20]%

4.2The Customer must claim a credit in writing within [30] days after the end of the month concerned. Credits are applied to the next invoice and are not paid in cash.

4.3Service credits in any month are capped at [20]% of that month's subscription fee. Service credits are the Customer's sole remedy for failing to meet the Availability Target, except where the Customer terminates under clause 5.

5.Chronic failure

If availability is below [98.0]% in any [three] months in a rolling twelve-month period, the Customer may terminate the affected Order Form by written notice within [30] days and receive a refund of prepaid fees for the remaining term.

6.Status and reporting

HR Connect will publish service status and planned maintenance at [status page URL] and, on request, give the Customer a monthly availability report.
