Independent Contractor Agreement

For freelancers and consultants: the services, fees and invoicing, independent status and IP assignment, with a statement of work.

Sample template. Replace everything in [brackets], delete what doesn't apply, and have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer in your jurisdiction before use. It is not legal advice.

This Independent Contractor Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made on [date] between:

(1) [Company name], a company registered in [country] under number [company number], whose registered office is at [registered address] (“Company”)

(2) [Contractor name / Contractor company name] of [address], [company number if a company] (“Contractor”).

1.Services

1.1The Contractor will provide the services described in the Statement of Work in Schedule 1 (the “Services”). The parties may add further Statements of Work by signing them.

1.2The Contractor will provide the Services with reasonable skill and care, in line with good industry practice and any agreed timetable.

2.Term

This Agreement starts on [start date] and continues until [end date / completion of the Services], unless terminated earlier under clause 12.

3.Fees and invoicing

3.1The Company will pay the Contractor the fees in Schedule 1, which are [$[0.00] per hour / $[0.00] per day / a fixed fee of $[0.00] payable on the milestones in Schedule 1].

3.2The Contractor will invoice [monthly in arrears / on completion of each milestone], with a summary of the time spent or deliverables completed. The Company will pay undisputed invoices within [30] days.

3.3The Company will reimburse reasonable expenses only if approved in writing in advance and supported by receipts.

3.4The Contractor is responsible for its own taxes, social contributions, insurance and registrations in connection with the fees. [Fees exclude any applicable sales or value-added tax, which the Contractor will show on its invoices.]

4.Independent contractor status

4.1The Contractor is an independent contractor and not an employee, worker, partner or agent of the Company. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment relationship.

4.2The Contractor decides how, when and where to perform the Services, subject to meeting agreed deadlines and reasonable site and security rules. The Contractor provides its own equipment unless agreed otherwise.

4.3The Contractor is not entitled to salary, paid leave, benefits or any other employee rights from the Company.

4.4The Contractor may work for other clients during this Agreement, as long as this does not conflict with its duties under it.

4.5[The Contractor may send a suitably qualified substitute, with the Company's prior approval, which will not be unreasonably withheld. The Contractor remains responsible for the substitute.]

5.Deliverables and acceptance

Where Schedule 1 lists deliverables, the Company will review each one within [10] business days of delivery and either accept it or explain in writing how it does not meet the agreed requirements. The Contractor will fix any such issues at no extra cost.

6.Intellectual property

6.1All intellectual property in the deliverables and anything else the Contractor creates for the Company under this Agreement (“Work Product”) is assigned to the Company on creation, or on payment of the related fees [if preferred].

6.2The Contractor keeps ownership of tools, know-how and materials it owned before this Agreement or creates independently of it (“Contractor Materials”). If Contractor Materials are included in Work Product, the Contractor grants the Company a perpetual, royalty-free, worldwide licence to use them as part of the Work Product.

6.3The Contractor will not include third-party or open-source materials in Work Product without telling the Company first.

7.Confidentiality

The Contractor will keep the Company's confidential information secret, use it only to provide the Services, and return or delete it when this Agreement ends. This duty continues after the Agreement ends.

8.Data protection

If the Contractor processes personal data on the Company's behalf, it will do so only on the Company's instructions, keep it secure, and comply with applicable data protection law.

9.Warranties

The Contractor warrants that it has the right to enter into this Agreement, that the Work Product will be its original work, and that the Company's use of the Work Product will not infringe any third party's rights.

10.Insurance

[The Contractor will maintain professional indemnity insurance of at least $[0.00] and public liability insurance of at least $[0.00] during this Agreement, and provide evidence on request.]

11.Liability

Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss. Each party's total liability under this Agreement is limited to [the total fees paid and payable under it], except for breaches of clauses 6 and 7, fraud, and liability that cannot be limited by law.

12.Termination

12.1Either party may end this Agreement by giving [14] days' written notice.

12.2Either party may end it immediately by written notice if the other commits a material breach that is not remedied within [7] days of being notified.

12.3On termination, the Company will pay for Services properly performed up to the termination date, and the Contractor will deliver all Work Product in progress and return the Company's property and information.

13.Non-solicitation

For [12] months after this Agreement ends, neither party will directly solicit for employment any employee of the other party with whom it dealt under this Agreement, without the other party's consent. General job adverts are allowed.

14.General

14.1This Agreement is governed by the laws of [governing law jurisdiction]. The courts of [jurisdiction for disputes] have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising out of or in connection with it, unless the parties agree in writing to resolve a dispute by [mediation / arbitration under named rules].

14.2This Agreement and its Schedules are the entire agreement between the parties about its subject. Changes must be in writing and signed by both parties. Neither party may assign it without the other's consent.
