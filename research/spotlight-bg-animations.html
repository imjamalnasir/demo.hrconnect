Spotlight background animations

Dark, animated backgrounds for the home page "Solutions" spotlight, built from the Neural H logo idea: nodes joined by lines, with light pulses travelling from one node to the next. Each sample uses the real spotlight content so you can judge how well the text and product card read on top.

Neural Drift

Free-floating nodes drift slowly; lines fade in between close neighbours and light pulses hop from node to node. The most organic and "alive" option.

Gentle 3D tilt

Option 2I · based on 2F

Softer 3D tilt

A calmer version of 2F. Each H tilts only slightly in 3D (up to about 32°), zooms in and out just a little, and everything moves slowly: the tilt, the zoom, the drift and the travelling light dots. Like 2E and 2F, the left side stays nearly clear behind the text.

Cell 340pxTilt ±32° (2F: ±57°)About half 2F's speedZoom ±5% (2F: ±10%)Dots 40% slower
