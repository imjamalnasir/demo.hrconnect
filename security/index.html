Security & privacy

Your people data, protected

HR and payroll data is some of the most sensitive data a company holds. Here's how HR Connect protects it.

How we protect your data

Your data stays yours

Every company's data is kept separate. Each request is tied to your company through your secure sign-in, so one company can never see another's records.

Role-based permissions

Control access per module, per screen and per action, such as view, manage, approve and delete. Override permissions for individual users.

Secure sign-in

Passwords are hashed, never stored in plain text. Sessions use short-lived tokens that refresh securely.

Secure file storage

Documents and receipts are stored in secure cloud storage and shared only through short-lived, signed links.

Audit trail

Payroll runs and approvals keep a history of who did what and when.

Only the modules you use

Modules can be switched on or off for your company, so data is only collected where you need it.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

HR Connect runs on established cloud infrastructure. Contact us for details of hosting regions.

Is AI trained on my data?

Your policies and receipts are used to answer your questions and fill in your forms. See our privacy policy for details of the AI providers we use.

Email hello@hrconnect.ai and we'll respond promptly.
