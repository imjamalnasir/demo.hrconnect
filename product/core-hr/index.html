How it works

Set up your structure

Add divisions, departments, designations and employee types, or use the defaults.

Add or import employees

Use the add-employee wizard for each person and invite them to the app.

Keep it current

Employees update their own details through self-service. HR approves what matters.

Frequently asked questions

Can employees edit their own profile?

Yes. In the Me workspace employees can view and update their own details. What they can change depends on the permissions you give their role.

Who can see salary and bank details?

Only roles you allow. Permissions are set per module and per action, and can be overridden for individual users.

Can I export the employee list?

Yes. HR can export the directory to Excel.
