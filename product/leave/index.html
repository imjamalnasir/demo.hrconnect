Leave that follows your rules

Configure leave once in your payroll setup. HR Connect does the counting.

Leave types ready to use

Annual, sick, maternity, paternity, emergency and bereavement, plus your own.

Working days counted for you

Requests count working days using your weekend days, so a request that spans a weekend is counted correctly, whichever days your weekend falls on.

Simple approvals

Approve to deduct the balance. Reject with a reason so the employee knows why.

Attachments

Employees can attach up to four files, such as a medical note.

Carry-over

Unused annual leave rolls into the next year under your carry-over rules. HR can preview it before year-end.

Clear balances

Allocated, used, carried over and remaining days for every leave type.

How it works

Set your leave rules

Choose leave types, days per year, weekend days and carry-over in your payroll setup.

Employees request leave

They pick dates and a type, see working days counted, and submit.

Approve and track

Approvals update balances straight away, and carry-over runs at year-end.

Frequently asked questions

Can I add my own leave types?

Yes. Add custom leave types alongside the standard ones in HR settings.

Does it handle different work weeks?

Yes. Weekend days are set per payroll setup, so teams with different schedules can each have their own.

Can HR apply leave on behalf of an employee?

Yes. HR can create, approve or reject requests for any employee they manage.

Try our free leave balance calculator.
