Built for how payroll really runs

Every step is tracked, from calculation to payment.

Payroll setup wizard

Choose country and currency, add earnings and deductions, and set leave and final pay rules.

Recalculate any time

Open runs can be recalculated as often as you like. Before submitting you see exactly what changed.

Approval flow

Runs move from open to pending approval, approved and paid. Rejections need a reason and go back for editing.

Pro-rating for new joiners

Employees who join mid-month are paid for the days they worked, automatically.

Bank payment file

Export the bank advice file as CSV or XLSX to pay employees through your bank.

Payslips

Paying a run issues payslips that employees can view and download as PDF.

Everything in one run

Loans, expenses and final settlements, included

You don't need to add them by hand. When a run is calculated, HR Connect brings in everything that's due that month.

  • Loan disbursements and monthly installments
  • Approved expense claims and cash advances
  • One-off earnings and deductions, such as bonuses or commission
  • Final settlements for employees leaving that month

Run payroll in four steps

Create the run

Pick the payroll setup and month. HR Connect calculates every employee straight away.

Review and adjust

Add one-off earnings or deductions, then recalculate.

Submit for approval

An approver checks the totals and approves or rejects with a reason.

Pay and issue payslips

Mark the run paid. Payslips go out, and loans, expenses and final settlements update.

Frequently asked questions

How often can I run payroll?

Payroll runs monthly. Each payroll setup has one run per month. Weekly, biweekly and semimonthly pay schedules aren't available yet.

Can I pay staff in different countries or currencies?

Yes. Create a payroll setup for each country, currency or entity and assign employees to the right one.

Can I add my own earnings and deductions?

Yes. Add any earning or deduction as a component in your payroll setup, for example a bonus, an allowance or a benefits deduction.

Does HR Connect file taxes or pay employees?

No. HR Connect doesn't calculate or file payroll taxes or government returns, and it doesn't send payments. It produces a bank payment file (CSV or XLSX) that you use to pay employees through your bank.
